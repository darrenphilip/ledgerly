Private, offline-first budgeting and spending tracker for Android.

### Install
1. Download `ledgerly-1.1.0.apk` below.
2. Open it on your Android 8.0+ phone and allow installation from unknown sources.

Or: `adb install -r ledgerly-1.1.0.apk`

**Upgrading from 1.0.0:** if the APK installs over your existing app, your data is kept.
If Android reports a signature conflict, the release key changed — uninstall 1.0.0 first,
which removes its data.

### What's new

**50:30:20 budgeting (opt-in)**
- Settings → Budget style → `50:30:20`, then set your monthly income.
- Ledgerly splits it into 50% needs / 30% wants / 20% savings and shows a plan card on the
  Budgets tab: target, spent, what's left (or what's over), and a plain-language verdict.
- Every category belongs to a bucket; a new **Savings** category makes the 20% measurable —
  log a transfer as a Savings expense and it counts.
- Simple mode (one limit per category) is unchanged and remains the default.

**Indonesian Rupiah**
- IDR added, and it is the default currency for new installs.
- Proper formatting: `Rp 1.234.567`, compact labels `Rp 12rb` / `Rp 10jt`.
- Entry understands how rupiah is written — `50000`, `50.000` and `50,000` all mean
  Rp 50.000 — with a live preview of the amount that will be saved.
- Existing currencies format exactly as before.

**Also**
- Release builds now ship with an empty ledger: demo data, the sample-data switch and the
  prefilled budget are debug-only.
- Stat tiles no longer clip long amounts.

### What is included
- Home dashboard with safe-to-spend, budget alerts and a category donut
- Activity list with search, filters and swipe-to-delete with undo
- Monthly and per-category budgets, plus the 50:30:20 plan
- Insights: 6-month trend, category breakdown and automatic highlights
- Add/edit sheet with amount pad, emoji categories and date picker
- Light/dark themes, 19 currencies including IDR, CSV export
- No account, no cloud, no permissions — data stays on device
