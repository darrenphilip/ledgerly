Private, offline-first budgeting and spending tracker for Android.

### Install
1. Download `ledgerly-1.5.0.apk` below.
2. Open it on your Android 8.0+ phone and allow installation from unknown sources.

Or: `adb install -r ledgerly-1.5.0.apk`

**Upgrading:** install straight over the top — same signing key, database migrates in place.
From 1.3.0 on, Ledgerly offers the update itself, so you don't need this page.

### What's new

**Ledgerly now taps you on the shoulder**
- **Daily reminder** (off by default): an evening nudge to log the day. It reads your ledger
  first — if you already logged something it says so ("You've logged Rp 87.500 today.
  Anything else?"), and if the day is empty it just asks.
- **Weekly review** (off by default): a one-line summary of the last seven days.
- **Preview a reminder** in Settings shows the notification immediately, so you know what
  you're signing up for.

**Payments from your notifications**
- With your permission, Ledgerly watches bank and wallet notifications for amounts and turns
  them into suggestions: a card on Home reading **Rp 87.500 · payment**, with category chips and
  a single **Add** button.
- It is deliberately hard to fool. The text needs both an amount *and* a payment word; OTPs,
  promos and dates are ignored. Reads "Rp 87.500", "1.250.000", QRIS, transfer, tagihan, gaji,
  refund, debit/kredit in Indonesian and English.
- **Nothing is written to your ledger until you tap Add**, and the suggestion is forgotten as
  soon as you add or dismiss it. Dismiss the card and it never comes back.
- Turn it off in Settings and the service stops reading anything. See the README's privacy
  section for exactly what is stored.

**Back button behaves**
- Pressing back while a keyboard is open used to close the whole sheet and lose what you were
  typing. It now closes the keyboard first; press back again to close the sheet. Fixed on every
  sheet and editor in the app.

### What is included
- Home dashboard with safe-to-spend, budget alerts and a category donut
- Activity list with search, filters and swipe-to-delete with undo
- Monthly and per-category budgets, month-specific overrides, payday cycles, 50:30:20 plan
- Custom categories with needs/wants/savings buckets
- Insights: 6-month trend, category breakdown and automatic highlights
- Add/edit sheet with amount pad, bucket labels and date picker
- Recurring entries, backup/restore, CSV export and import
- Reminders, payment suggestions from notifications, app lock
- Light/dark themes, 19 currencies including IDR
- In-app updates from the public release feed
- No account, no cloud — data stays on device
