Private, offline-first budgeting and spending tracker for Android.

### Install
1. Download `ledgerly-1.4.0.apk` below.
2. Open it on your Android 8.0+ phone and allow installation from unknown sources.

Or: `adb install -r ledgerly-1.4.0.apk`

**Upgrading:** install straight over the top. Same signing key, and the database migrates in
place, so your ledger, budgets, categories and settings are kept. If you're on 1.3.0 or later
you don't need this page — Ledgerly offers the update itself.

### What's new

**Every expense now sorts itself**
- Categories decide whether spending counts as a **need**, a **want** or a **saving**, and the
  50:30:20 plan uses that automatically. You never assign a bucket per transaction.
- **Make your own categories** in Settings → Categories: name, icon, colour, and which pot it
  counts as. A "Gym" category marked as a need lands in needs; "Investing" marked as savings
  counts toward your 20%.
- Built-in categories can be **renamed, re-sorted into a different pot, or hidden** — history
  keeps its labels either way. Deleting is only offered for categories you created.
- The amount pad now shows the pot under each category (`need`, `want`, `save`), so the split
  is never a mystery.

**A real setup flow**
- Onboarding is now four short steps instead of one dense form: welcome → name and currency →
  budget style → the frame of your month.
- Pick **Simple** or **50:30:20** up front; if you pick 50:30:20 you enter your income and see
  the split calculated live (Rp 10.000.000 → 5/3/2).
- **Your month can start on your payday.** If your salary lands on the 25th, set that day and
  every total, budget and plan runs 25 Sep – 24 Oct instead of the calendar month.
- The last step restates everything you chose before you commit, and you can skip any step.
- Back, progress and skip are consistent on every step.

**Also**
- Existing installs get your current categories as they were — nothing is re-bucketed behind
  your back.
- Backups include custom categories, so restoring on a new phone brings them with it.

### What is included
- Home dashboard with safe-to-spend, budget alerts and a category donut
- Activity list with search, filters and swipe-to-delete with undo
- Monthly and per-category budgets, month-specific overrides, payday cycles, 50:30:20 plan
- Custom categories with needs/wants/savings buckets
- Insights: 6-month trend, category breakdown and automatic highlights
- Add/edit sheet with amount pad, bucket labels and date picker
- Recurring entries, backup/restore, CSV export and import
- Light/dark themes, 19 currencies including IDR, app lock
- In-app updates from the public release feed
- No account, no cloud — data stays on device
